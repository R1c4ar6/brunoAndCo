# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Dog owners in Granada, Spain — a mix of busy professionals, families, and elderly owners — who need reliable, caring help with dog walking and at-home pet care when they cannot be present.

## Product Purpose

Bruno Paseos & Cuidados is a local pet care service website that generates trust and contact requests from dog owners in Granada. Success means visitors understand the service, feel confident in the care, and reach out to request information or book.

## Positioning

A personal, trust-first pet care service built around a real dog (Bruno) and a real person, offering a warm, neighborhood feel that large platforms and apps cannot replicate. The service emphasizes personal attention, local knowledge of Granada, and genuine affection for dogs.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from social media, word-of-mouth, or local search. They browse a single-page site on mobile or desktop, read about the service and pricing, check testimonials, and contact via phone, WhatsApp, email, or Instagram. No online booking or payment — all scheduling happens through direct contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page marketing website (HTML/CSS only, no JavaScript, no frameworks)
- Four core services: daily walks (30/60 min), at-home care, feeding visits, companionship
- Simple pricing: 15€ (30 min), 20€ (60 min), at-home care by quote
- Contact-only model (no e-commerce, no booking system)
- All content in Spanish
- Placeholder images need real photos of Bruno and the service
- Placeholder contact information needs real phone, WhatsApp, email, and Instagram

## Brand Commitments

- Business name: Bruno Paseos & Cuidados
- Tagline: "Cuidamos a tu mejor amigo como si fuera parte de nuestra familia."
- Accent color: #4CAF50 (green)
- Mascot and main image: Bruno the dog (real photos pending)
- Voice: warm, friendly, trustworthy, professional but never corporate
- Location: Granada, España

## Evidence on Hand

- Complete single-page website (index.html + styles.css) with all sections built
- Placeholder images for Bruno photos (dashed-border blocks ready for replacement)
- Placeholder contact information (phone, WhatsApp, email, Instagram)
- Fake testimonials from María, Carlos, and Lucía

## Product Principles

1. Trust through personal connection — Bruno is the face, not a brand abstraction
2. Local expertise — knowledge of Granada's best dog-friendly spots and routes
3. Transparency — clear pricing, no hidden fees, real testimonials
4. Simplicity — easy to understand, easy to contact, no friction
5. Reliability — consistent, caring service that dog owners can depend on

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, alt attributes on all images, good color contrast ratios, responsive design for all screen sizes.
